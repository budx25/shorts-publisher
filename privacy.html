Privacy Policy

shorts-publisher · last updated 12 November 2026

Summary

shorts-publisher is a personal tool that runs only on its owner's own computer and is used only with the owner's own accounts. It has no servers, no users other than its owner, and collects nothing about anyone else.

What it accesses

  • TikTok: basic profile information (display name), to confirm the right account is connected, and permission to upload videos to the owner's inbox as drafts (user.info.basic, video.upload).
  • YouTube and Instagram: permission to upload videos to the owner's own channel or account.

Where data is kept

Sign-in tokens are stored in a local file on the owner's computer and are sent only to the platform they belong to. Videos are sent only to the platform the owner chooses. Nothing is stored on any other server, sold, or shared with third parties, and no analytics or tracking are used.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time in each platform's account settings (on TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services). Deleting the local token file removes all stored data.
